## what you get

  • profile — your top artists, recent tracks, playlists. customize colors, fonts, banners, badges, particles, custom css.
  • discover — find people by genre or scroll and see who's online. view counts. leaderboards.
  • chat — global chat room. talk to everyone online right now.
  • forums — discuss music, report bugs, share finds. threaded discussions.
  • friends — see what your people are playing. no likes, no feeds, just music.
  • stats — hours, genre breakdown, obscure taste score, profile views, hearts.
  • links — connect every platform. 30+ social links. one page.
  • guestbook — let people leave comments on your profile.
  • export — your data is yours. back it up. move it. no lock-in.
